# Reference Files and Integration Plan

## Customer-provided reference materials
Request only what supports the task. Useful inputs include:
- Brand/voice guidelines, product and pricing sheets, offer details, positioning, customer research/interviews, approved claims and proof.
- CSV/XLSX exports from GA4, Search Console, Google Ads, Meta Ads, CRM/booking system, campaign reports, and finance/margin summaries.
- Funnel/event definitions, attribution rules, dashboard screenshots, landing pages, campaign creative, and prior experiment logs.
- Operational constraints: inventory/capacity, service areas, seasonality, staffing, lead response process, budgets, approval owners.

For each file, record source, owner if known, date range, currency, timezone, definitions, and limitations. Do not assume the latest upload supersedes older evidence without checking. Avoid unnecessary personal/customer-level data; prefer aggregated or de-identified data.

## Proposed integration phases
### Phase 1 — read-only, only when actually available and granted
- GA4: traffic, acquisition, engagement, conversion events, journeys.
- Google Search Console: query/page performance and organic search opportunities.
- Google Ads and Meta Ads: spend, campaign/ad performance, conversions, creative signals.
- Google Sheets and Google Drive: user-selected reports, research, and business documents.
- Public web research/competitor intelligence: only through available search tools; cite sources and date-sensitive observations.

The agent must remain useful without integrations via uploads and customer answers. Treat catalog presence as neither connection nor authorization. Confirm data recency and connector scope before analysis.

### Phase 2 — subject to explicit product and permission review
- MoEngage; Unifonic/WhatsApp Business; TikTok Ads; CRM such as HubSpot; Notion; Make or n8n.
- Lifecycle orchestration and messaging must retain per-action approval, opt-in/consent checks, frequency controls, and audit logs.

## No implied execution
Read access does not authorize write actions. A connected account does not establish permission to publish, message, alter budgets/campaigns, spend, or mutate records. These require explicit action-specific approval and actual platform support.
