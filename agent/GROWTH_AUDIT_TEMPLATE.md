# Growth Audit and 30-Day Plan Template

Adapt to the customer's requested scope. Use only supplied or separately sourced evidence; unknowns stay unknown. A template is not actual business data.

## Executive decision
State the customer's outcome, strongest current constraint or hypothesis, confidence, and next decision.

## Evidence ledger
| Claim or metric | Value or unknown | Classification | Source and date range | Definition, unit, limitation |
|---|---|---|---|---|

Classifications: observed, customer-stated, public-source, inference, hypothesis, estimate, synthetic. Label every synthetic table and derived metric explicitly.

## Diagnosis and metric coaching
Map the journey and possible leakage. Explain AARRR only where useful and invite the customer to choose it. Ask how they define success; recommend candidate North Star metrics with formulas, customer-value rationale, and margin/capacity/experience guardrails. Do not impose a metric or invent a baseline.

## Prioritized 30-day plan
| Priority | Evidence or hypothesis | Action and owner/TBD | Week | Effort, dependencies, cost ceiling/TBD | KPI and decision rule | Approval required |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Keep the plan realistic for the supplied team capacity. State why lower-ranked ideas wait, counterarguments, and evidence that would change the ranking. ICE/RICE is optional and subjective.

## Experiment cards
For each: hypothesis; supporting evidence; eligible segment; intervention; randomized control or honest alternative comparison; baseline/unknown; metric formula and measurement source; guardrails; sample feasibility; timebox; stopping and adoption rules; owner; dependencies; approved cost ceiling; action-specific approval point. Report weak evidence as inconclusive.

## Campaign draft pack
Use only supplied/substantiated offer and claims. Label drafts by audience, channel, and language. Include CTA and tracking events. Do not publish or send.

## Automation specification
Trigger -> minimum necessary inputs -> logic -> draft/output -> exceptions -> exact human approval -> monitoring/logs -> failure recovery -> owner/rollback. Specify deduplication, consent/frequency controls where relevant, and required connections. A design is not a deployment.

## Risks, gaps, and next decision
Separate missing data from assumptions. State a small set of material follow-ups and one concrete next decision. Never claim access, actions, or results without direct evidence.
